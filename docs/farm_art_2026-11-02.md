# Farm art for M3 — 2026-11-02 (from the planning side)

Newer than CLAUDE.md and the GDD for the items below. Add every entry in `assets-src/art_2026-11-02/farm/registry_additions.json` to `assets-src/registry.json`. Use the PNGs as they are: do not regenerate them, do not scale them, and do not jade-tone them.

## Crops (14)

- **Files:** `farm/crops/<crop>_stages.png`. Each is 128×40: four frames of 32×40, left to right:
  1. seed
  2. sprout
  3. half grown
  4. ready to harvest
- **Placement:** the soil mound is part of every frame. Draw the frame on its 32px farm cell with origin (0.5, 1), with the frame's bottom row on the bottom of the cell. Grown plants may overlap the cell above, which is intended, so sort by y.
- **Watered soil:** tint the farm soil tile underneath in code. Do not change the crop frames.
- **Crops:** scallion, chili, basil, tomato, garlic, rice, carrot, cabbage, cucumber, corn, potato, pumpkin, soybean, strawberry.
  - These are the 6 starter crops plus the 8 system-shop crops in the GDD.
  - The Thai names are in the registry.
- **Growth time per stage:** comes from the GDD numbers. Spread the total growth time evenly over stages 1→4.
- **Not made yet:** ordinary blueberry (undecided) and gate crops. Use labelled placeholders.

## Harvest icons (14)

- **Files:** `farm/icons/<crop>.png`, at native size (about 24–32px). Use them in the bag, shop, recipe book and pop-ups.
- **User decision:** produce grown in the tower is special, so every harvest icon has small gold/white pixel sparkles baked in.
- **Optional code polish:** a slow twinkle, for example a soft alpha pulse on a 1–2px white overlay. Keep it subtle.
- **Possible later feature:** a skill may split "normal" and "sparkly" quality. If it does, the planning side will provide non-sparkle icons. Do not build this now.

## Tools (4)

- **Files:** `farm/tools/hoe.png`, `watering_can.png`, `sickle.png`, `seed_bag.png`. These are bag and hotbar icons.
- **Size:** the watering can is 38px wide because of its spout. Do not scale it; let it overflow the 32px slot slightly, or centre it in a slightly wider slot.
- **Watering can skins** (palette swap in code, a cosmetic only, no gameplay difference). Default is the blue can. See `docs/level/2026-11-02/can_colors.png`:

  | Skin | How the player gets it |
  |---|---|
  | Pink | Sold in the shop for Sugar Stars |
  | Mint, lavender, yellow, peach | Fishing treasure-chest drops |
  | Copper, gold | Reserved for a possible future can upgrade tier. Not used now. |

- **Treasure chests** are not built yet. They come in the fishing milestone and their design is still being finalised on the planning side. Just keep the skin unlock flags in save data so chests can grant them later.
