# Crop sprites fixed: 48×64 frames (2026-11-02 night)

Problem the user found: grown crops were cut off. The generated plants were wider/taller than the 32×40 cell, so stage 3–4 of many crops had a straight cut on the left/right edge (strawberry, potato, pumpkin, chili, corn, blueberry...) and a flat top.

Fix (planning side):
- Rebuilt every crop sheet from the original generations with the full plant (0 credits).
- Tomato, chili and corn stage 4 had their tops cut by the generation canvas; their tops were regenerated with PixelLab inpaint (6 credits); potato, cucumber and rice tops likewise (6 credits); garlic, scallion and basil kept their own leaves with hand-tapered tips. All approved by the user.
- **Frame size is now 48 wide × 64 tall.** Sheets: `public/assets/farm/crops48/<crop>_stages.png`, 192×64 = 4 frames (stage 1–4). All 15 crops incl. blueberry. (The folder name stays crops48.)
- Withered: `public/assets/farm/crops48/withered/withered_<crop>.png`, 48×64.
- The old 32×40 cell sits at (8, 24) inside each 48×64 frame, so the soil mound is exactly where it was: bottom row of the frame = bottom of the plot cell, horizontally centred. Keep origin (0.5, 1) at the cell's bottom centre.
- Grown plants overlap the plots above and beside them a little; depth-sort by y as before.
- Switch the crop spritesheets (registry / preload) to these files with frameWidth 48, frameHeight 64, then stop using `farm/crops/*_stages.png` and `farm/crops/withered/`.
