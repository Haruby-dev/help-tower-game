# Phone v3 (2026-11-02, late). This REPLACES the "phone open" and "phone button" parts of docs/ui_update_2026-11-02.md

The book-style open frame (`ui/phone_open_frame.png`) and the pink cat phone button (`ui/phone_closed.png`) are **superseded**. Do not build them. Everything else in ui_update_2026-11-02.md still applies: the joystick, speech bubbles, app icons, no coin pill, Mali font and opaque UI.

The new design is a cute foldable phone, like the iPhone Duo. The final art will come from the planning side. Until then, build the layout with placeholders that follow these mockups in `docs/level/2026-11-02/`.

1. **Corner button = mini lock screen** (`ui34_scene_lockmini.png`).
   - A cream-rimmed phone at the bottom-right showing a sky wallpaper with clouds and two sleeping loaf cats, a weather cat icon, "พุธ 5" and the time in big text. Tap it to open the phone.
2. **Lock screen** (`ui33_lock_v2.png`, `ui35_scene_lockbig.png`). It rises to the centre of the screen and the world dims.
   - Big time.
   - Day and weather, with weather cat icons for sun, cloud, rain and storm.
   - Sleeping cats on the sky wallpaper: the orange hometown cat, calico, Gio and grey.
   - A daily encouragement card with Gio's face, e.g. "วันนี้ทำได้แค่ไหน ก็พอแล้วนะ". Keep the lines in data and pick one at random each day.
   - A paw round button.
   - Tap or swipe up to go to the home screen.
3. **Home screen, folded** (`ui27_folded.png`):
   - A date widget with a week strip and today highlighted.
   - A 4-column grid of the sticker app icons.
   - A big Gio widget.
   - A vertical dock on the right with wallet, mail and settings.
   - A punch-hole and the time, plus page dots.
4. **Unfolded** (`ui28_unfolded.png`): wide, with a faint centre crease. Use it for big apps (wallet, recipe book, calendar) or open it with a button. It shows:
   - the date widget, apps, and a "งานวันนี้" checklist
   - the tower calendar with today's event
   - the "ติดอยู่ในหอคอย" counter (months, days, hours since day 1, plus floor N/100)
   - the dock
5. **Flip to the back** (`ui30_flip.png`, `ui29_backs.png`).
   - A button flips the phone. Animate it by squashing the X scale in code.
   - The back shows **2 cameras** and the equipped **case skin**.
   - Cases: pink cat (default), Gio face, strawberry milk and mint choc-chip. More can be added; they are cosmetic and come from the shop or chests.
   - Strap charms are a separate cosmetic slot.
6. **Wallpapers** are collectible too, e.g. a sky with cats, or Gio. Add a cosmetic slot for them.

Draw it all in the soft pastel pixel style of the mockups. Mali text with a dark outline is fine. 0 PixelLab calls.
