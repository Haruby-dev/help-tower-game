# Farm art part 2 (2026-11-02): seed bags and gardening animations, for M3

Add everything in `assets-src/art_2026-11-02/farm/registry_additions_2.json`. Use the PNGs as they are, with no scaling and no jade tone.

## Seed bags (14)
- **Files:** `farm/seeds/seed_<crop>.png`, 23×27.
- **Look:** the chosen seed-bag tool icon, tinted a little per crop, with a small crop label on the front. They have NO sparkles, so they don't look like the harvested produce.
- **Use:** shop, bag and planting picker.

## Player gardening animations (girl, 3 directions + mirror)
- **Files:** `farm/anims/<anim>_<dir>.png`. Each is a horizontal strip of 9 frames at 88×88, the same canvas and anchor as walk-v3.
- **Directions:** south, east and north. West = east flipped.

| Animation | Frames |
|---|---|
| `plant` | Crouch, drop a seed, pat the soil twice (frames 4–7), stand up |
| `water` | Tilt the watering can and pour |
| `harvest` | Crouch, pull the plant, lift it up happily |
| `till` | Raise the hoe and dig |

- **Weak spots the user accepted for now:** south water (the can is small and pale), south till (the tool reads like a sickle) and north harvest (the pull is hidden). Cover them with code effects.

## Code effects (user-approved "veggies wake up" idea, see `docs/level/2026-11-02/story_farm.png`)
- **Plant:** small dirt puffs on each pat frame, then a sprout pops up with a bounce.
- **Water:** a droplet particle arc in front of the can, and the soil tile darkens. A sleeping sprout shows "Zzz", then stretches (scaleY 1.3 → 1) and gets a tiny sparkle: it "wakes up".
- **Fully grown crop:** a gentle idle bounce means "ready".
- **Harvest:** the produce pops out of the soil in an arc with a sparkle trail into the player's hands, then "+1 <name>".
- **Till:** dirt chips fly on the hit frames (frames 4–6).

Timing: about 110–140 ms per frame. Lock movement during an action, but let a new tap queue the next one.
