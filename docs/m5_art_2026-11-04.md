## Bobber (she asked for a round float, 0 credits)
- **Files:** `fx/bobber_idle_4f.png`, `fx/bobber_nibble_4f.png`, `fx/bobber_bite_4f.png`. Each is 4 frames of 16×16 with a round red/white float.
- **Pivot:** the water line is at y = 11 inside the frame, centred at x = 8. Place that point on the water surface at the cast spot.
- **Antenna tip moves with the float** (approved 2026-11-04). Attach the line to the tip of the current frame: idle (8,3) (8,3) (8,4) (8,4); nibble (8,4) (8,5) (8,4) (8,3); bite (8,6) (8,8) (8,9) (8,9).
- **Line:** draw the fishing line from the rod tip in the `fish_*` frame to the bobber's antenna tip (per-frame list above).
- **Playback:**
  - Waiting: loop `idle` at ~4 fps.
  - Before the "!": play `nibble` 1–2 times at 6 fps.
  - When the "!" shows: play `bite` once at 8 fps, then hold its last frame until the tap or timeout.
  - Caught or escaped: hide the bobber, and reel the line in with a short code tween.
